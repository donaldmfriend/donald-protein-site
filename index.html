Donald Protein

Donald Protein is a personal Android app that logs food and protein into the owner's own Google Sheets protein tracker. It shows a daily dashboard of protein eaten against a goal and writes new entries into the signed-in user's spreadsheet.

The app connects to Google Sheets only to read and write the spreadsheet that the user chooses. It has no server and no accounts of its own.
